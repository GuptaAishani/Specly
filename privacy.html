Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you use Specly, we may process your email address, authentication identifiers, subscription status, Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, and technical information needed to operate the service. Payment card details are collected and processed by Stripe; Specly does not receive your full card number.

2. Project data

Some project notes, design values, and revisions may be stored locally in your browser rather than on Specly’s servers. If a feature explicitly sends project data to a server, the interface will indicate that behavior.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, provide paid access, operate and secure the service, process subscription events, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and understand whether the product is functioning correctly.

4. Service providers

Specly currently relies on third-party providers including Stripe for payments, Supabase for authentication/database/Edge Functions, and GitHub Pages for hosting. Those providers process information on our behalf or as independent service providers according to their own privacy terms.

5. Cookies and local storage

Specly and its authentication providers may use browser storage or cookies to maintain sign-in sessions and product functionality. Specly also uses local browser storage to save certain project work on your device.

6. Analytics

Specly may use privacy-respecting or standard web analytics to understand visits, feature use, and conversion performance. If analytics are enabled, this Privacy Policy should be updated to name the provider and describe any cookies or identifiers it uses.

7. Retention

We retain account and subscription records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and protect against fraud. Local project data remains on your device until you or your browser removes it.

8. Your choices

You may cancel your subscription through Stripe’s billing portal. You may also contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.

9. Children

Specly is not intentionally directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards appropriate to an early-stage web service, including authenticated access controls and server-side storage of payment secrets. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as our product, providers, or legal obligations change. The date above shows the most recent revision.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact information on the Contact page.

Before launch: If you add Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, session replay, email marketing, or other tracking, update this page before enabling those tools.
