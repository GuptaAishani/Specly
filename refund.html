Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

First 3 days free

Eligible new customers receive their first 3 days free. A payment method is required. Cancel through “Manage billing” before the displayed introductory-access end time if you do not want the first US$15 monthly charge.

Monthly subscription charges

After the first 3 days, Specly Studio renews at US$15 per month plus applicable tax until canceled. You can cancel future renewal through Stripe’s customer billing portal from your Specly account.

Refund requests

Subscription charges are generally non-refundable once a billing period has begun, except where required by law. If you believe you were charged because of a billing error, duplicate charge, unauthorized transaction, or a material service failure, contact us promptly through the Contact page. We will review the circumstances and may issue a full or partial refund at our discretion where permitted by law.

Cancellation timing

Canceling a paid subscription normally stops the next renewal. Unless Stripe’s portal states otherwise, cancellation does not retroactively refund the current billing period and access may remain available until that period ends.

Chargebacks

If you do not recognize a charge, please contact us first so we can investigate. Nothing here limits any rights you may have with your card issuer or under applicable law.

Taxes

Taxes collected and remitted as part of a transaction may be handled differently depending on applicable law and Stripe’s processing rules.
