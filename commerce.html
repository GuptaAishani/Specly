A few things to know

How is payment handled?

Checkout and billing are handled securely by Stripe. Specly does not receive or store your full card number.

What happens after the first 3 days?

Your saved payment method is charged US$15 plus applicable tax, then every month until you cancel. Your account shows when the introductory 3-day period ends. Cancel from Manage billing before that time to avoid the first charge.

Can I keep my projects after canceling?

Downloaded reports and project files remain yours. Projects stored in your browser are not deleted by cancellation. Export backups before your access ends; browser storage can be cleared.

Is passing a test an engineering approval?

No. A pass only means a design meets the stated analytical model and assumptions. Real hardware needs appropriate drawings, materials, testing and review.
