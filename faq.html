Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated September 25, 2026

What is Specly?

Specly is a guided engineering project studio designed to help students build portfolio evidence they can explain in internship and job interviews.

What do I get with membership?

Specly Studio includes 45 member projects across beginner, intermediate, and advanced difficulty. Each member project includes an industry-inspired RFP, a real-world engineering problem, numerical success criteria, required work products, an interactive analytical model, engineering notes, a proposed validation plan, and an exportable portfolio report.

How much does it cost?

Specly Studio is US$15 per month plus applicable tax. Eligible new accounts get their first 3 days free after completing Stripe Checkout.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Use “Manage billing” in your account to open Stripe’s customer portal and cancel future renewal.

Can I put a Specly project on my resume?

Yes. Treat it as a self-directed or Specly-guided engineering project and be accurate about the scope. Show the problem, requirement matrix, design iterations, calculations, margins, and validation plan you personally developed. Do not imply that the fictional stakeholder commissioned you, that you worked for the named industry, or that you built and tested hardware unless you actually did.

Is Specly professional engineering software?

No. Specly uses educational analytical models. It is not CAD, FEA, engineering certification, or a substitute for hardware testing and qualified engineering review.

Where are my projects saved?

Current project revisions are saved locally in your browser. Export reports you want to keep because browser storage can be cleared.

Will more projects be added?

Yes. The project library is designed to keep growing, with new projects added consistently. Personalized project briefs are also coming soon.

How do I sign back in later?

Use the Specly sign-in page with your email and password. Active members are returned to the member studio without going through checkout again. If your account was originally created with Specly’s older email-link sign in, use Forgot password once to create a password for that same account.

How are payments handled?

Stripe handles checkout, card storage, billing, and the customer portal. Specly does not receive your full card number.
